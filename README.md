## Projects

### 🎓 [Campus Connect](https://github.com/RishitVerma31/campus_connect)
A student networking platform with OTP login, personalised profiles, swipe-based matching communities and real-time group chat. It's live at [ourcampusconnect.in](https://www.ourcampusconnect.in).
`Python` `Flask` `Firebase` `Firestore` `JavaScript`

### 🎤 [MockInterview AI](https://github.com/RishitVerma31/MockInterview)
A full-stack mock interview platform. Groq AI writes role-specific questions and evaluates answers, interviews run by voice, and live proctoring checks head pose, face visibility and extra people. You get scores, feedback and sample answers. [Live demo](https://mock-interview-six-delta.vercel.app).
`React` `JavaScript` `Python` `Groq AI` `Web Speech API`

### 🤖 [J.A.R.V.I.S.](https://github.com/RishitVerma31/jarvis)
A locally hosted, voice-controlled personal AI assistant with wake-word detection, Faster-Whisper speech recognition, local LLM replies and Kokoro TTS. It uses WebSockets and Tailscale for remote access, and Qwen2.5-VL with FFmpeg for timestamped video analysis.
`Python` `FastAPI` `React` `Ollama` `Faster-Whisper` `Qwen2.5-VL` `FFmpeg` `Tailscale`
